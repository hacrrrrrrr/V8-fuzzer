# Sponsorship & Research Support

## V8-Fuzzer

V8-Fuzzer is an independent open-source project focused on JavaScript engine fuzzing, crash discovery, minimization, and root-cause research.

The project is designed to make engine-security research practical on modest hardware while providing a professional, reproducible corpus and triage workflow.

### What support helps fund

- hardware for longer fuzzing campaigns
- sanitizer/debug V8 builds
- storage for corpus and crash archives
- research documentation
- maintenance and new fuzzing modules
